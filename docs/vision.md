# Project Vision

## Overview

This website is an art-focused digital gallery and commerce platform.

The goal is not to behave like a traditional e-commerce website.

The website should evoke emotion, calmness, contemplation, and connection.

Visitors should feel they are entering an artist's living journal and gallery space.

Primary artworks:

- Silk Paintings
- Sculptural Paintings
- Buddhist Artworks
- Commissioned Works

---

## Brand Positioning

Handcrafted contemporary artworks inspired by stillness, mindfulness, nature and meaningful living.

---

## Core Goals

### Business

- Sell original artworks
- Accept commission requests
- Build collector trust
- Increase organic traffic through content

### Experience

- Slow browsing experience
- Minimal distractions
- High visual impact
- Emotional storytelling

---

## Audience

### Primary

Art collectors

### Secondary

Interior designers

### Third

People interested in mindfulness, Buddhism, slow living and home decor
