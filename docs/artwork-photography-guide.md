# Artwork Photography Guide

## Goal

Collectors buy artwork through trust.

Photography must communicate:

* Texture
* Scale
* Material
* Atmosphere
* Craftsmanship

---

# Required Photos For Every Artwork

## 1. Hero Image

Front-facing artwork.

Straight angle.

Neutral lighting.

No distractions.

Purpose:

Primary sales image.

---

## 2. Texture Detail

Close-up photography.

Capture:

* Brush strokes
* Sculptural texture
* Silk fibers
* Surface depth

Purpose:

Show craftsmanship.

---

## 3. Side Angle

45-degree angle.

Reveal dimensionality.

Especially important for Sculptural Paintings.

---

## 4. Room View

Artwork installed in a realistic interior.

Purpose:

Help collectors imagine ownership.

Recommended styles:

* Japandi
* Modern Minimalist
* Contemporary

Avoid unrealistic AI interiors.

---

## 5. Scale Reference

Include:

* Chair
* Console table
* Person

Purpose:

Understand size.

---

## 6. Artist Studio Photo

Optional but recommended.

Show work in progress.

Build emotional connection.

---

# Video Guidelines

Every sculptural artwork should include a short video.

Duration:

10-30 seconds

Movement:

Slow pan

Natural light

Focus on texture

No music required.

Ambient studio sound preferred.

---

# Lighting

Preferred:

Soft daylight

Near window

Overcast sky

---

Avoid:

Direct flash

Harsh shadows

Colored lighting

---

# File Naming

Good:

lotus-in-silence-texture.jpg

lotus-in-silence-room-view.jpg

---

Bad:

IMG_2389.jpg

final-final.jpg

---

# Image Optimization

Master Files:

3000px+

Web Version:

1600px+

Format:

WebP

Quality:

80-85%

---

# Consistency Rules

All artworks should:

* Use similar lighting
* Use similar framing
* Use similar editing

The collection should feel cohesive.

Not assembled from unrelated photos.
