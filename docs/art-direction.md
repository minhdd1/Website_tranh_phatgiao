# Art Direction

## Core Principle

This website is not an online store.

It is a digital gallery.

Every design decision must support contemplation rather than consumption.

The artwork is always the hero.

The interface should disappear into the background.

---

# Emotional Direction

Visitors should feel:

* Calm
* Curious
* Inspired
* Connected
* Unhurried

The experience should resemble entering a quiet gallery on a peaceful afternoon.

---

# Visual References

Keywords:

* Contemporary Art Gallery
* Slow Living
* Editorial Design
* Luxury Minimalism
* Japanese Simplicity
* Scandinavian Warmth

Avoid:

* Commercial e-commerce aesthetics
* Bright promotional banners
* Flash sale visuals
* Discount labels
* Popups that interrupt browsing

---

# Layout Philosophy

Large imagery.

Generous whitespace.

Minimal text.

Slow visual rhythm.

Every section should have room to breathe.

---

# Image Ratios

Preferred:

4:5

Used for:

* Artwork cards
* Featured collections

---

Secondary:

3:4

Used for:

* Editorial imagery
* Artist portraits

---

Landscape:

16:9

Used sparingly:

* Hero banners
* Studio photography

---

# Typography Hierarchy

## Heading

Cormorant Garamond

Large.

Elegant.

Never bold-heavy.

Recommended:

48px – 80px desktop

32px – 48px mobile

---

## Body

Lora

Comfortable reading.

18px desktop

16px mobile

---

# White Space Rules

Never crowd artwork.

Minimum spacing:

64px desktop

32px mobile

between major sections.

Artwork cards should never touch one another visually.

---

# Homepage Mood

The homepage should feel like turning pages of an art book.

Not browsing a catalog.

Sequence:

Image

Silence

Story

Artwork

Reflection

Journal

---

# Color Usage

Background dominates.

Artwork provides color.

Interface should remain neutral.

Primary Background:
#FAF8F4

Secondary:
#EFE7DF

Accent:
#F3E6E4

Text:
#2F2F2F

---

# Animation

Extremely subtle.

Fade-in only.

Slow transitions:

300ms – 600ms

Avoid:

* Bounce
* Parallax
* Flying elements
* Auto sliders

---

# Product Page Direction

A single artwork should feel like an exhibition.

Sequence:

Artwork Image

Title

Story

Details

Material Closeups

Room View

Video Texture

Inquiry

The visitor should spend time with the piece before seeing its price.

---

# Mobile Experience

Mobile should preserve the gallery feeling.

Never compress artwork into tiny thumbnails.

Large images remain a priority.
